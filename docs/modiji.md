## Snapshot
- **Full name:** Narendra Damodardas Modi (often addressed as *Modiji*)
- **Born:** 17 September 1950, Vadnagar, Gujarat, India[^1]
- **Current roles:** Prime Minister of India (since 2014); Member of Parliament for Varanasi; leader of the National Democratic Alliance (NDA)[^2]
- **Affiliations:** Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP); Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS)[^1]
- **Signature themes:** Economic modernisation, welfare delivery, Hindu nationalist messaging, and a highly centralised leadership style[^8]

## Early Life and Political Rise
Raised in a family of modest means in Vadnagar, Modi helped at the family tea stall, joined the RSS as a teenager, and earned a Master of Arts in political science before transitioning to the BJP in 1987.[^1] Organisational roles during the 1990s culminated in his appointment as Gujarat chief minister in 2001, where he oversaw rapid industrial growth while navigating intense scrutiny for the 2002 communal riots.[^1]

## Third-Term Mandate (2024–2026)
The 2024 general election delivered Modi a rare third consecutive term, but the BJP secured 240 Lok Sabha seats—short of a solo majority—making coalition management with allies such as the Telugu Desam Party and Janata Dal (United) pivotal for NDA stability.[^2][^3] Modi retained key cabinet portfolios for the BJP to signal continuity while negotiating partner demands on infrastructure, welfare, and regional development priorities.[^3]

## Economic and Social Initiatives
Modi’s third-term agenda emphasises employment, industrial policy, and welfare delivery. The cabinet cleared a ₹99,446 crore Employment-Linked Incentive (ELI) scheme in July 2025 to subsidise first-time workers and incentivise firms to create 35 million jobs between August 2025 and July 2027, with extended benefits for manufacturing employers.[^4] In his Independence Day 2025 address, Modi announced the ₹1 lakh crore Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rojgar Yojana, offering ₹15,000 direct transfers for youth landing their first private-sector job alongside promises of simplified GST slabs and mission-mode semiconductor manufacturing.[^5]

## Defence, Security, and Foreign Policy
Following Operation Sindoor in May 2025—when Indian air and missile defences intercepted hundreds of Pakistani drones—Modi highlighted the integration of systems like Akash, Akashteer, and the S-400 within a layered security grid.[^7] On 15 August 2025 he unveiled the Sudarshan Chakra Mission, a ten-year Indigenous plan to build an Iron Dome–style shield that pairs air- and missile-defence layers with precision counterstrike capabilities for critical national infrastructure.[^6] India subsequently scheduled its largest counter-drone exercise, "Cold Start," for October 2025 to stress-test these capabilities against emerging threats.[^9]

## Leadership Style and Public Image
Supporters credit Modi with decisive leadership, welfare expansion, and elevating India’s global stature, while critics warn of democratic backsliding, centralised power, and increased pressures on minorities and dissenting media.[^8] The 2024 verdict, which reduced the BJP’s seat share, has intensified debates about balancing Modi’s personal brand with inclusive governance and institutional checks.[^2][^8]
