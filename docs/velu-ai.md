# Velou (Velu AI) Overview

_Last updated: October 30, 2025_

Velou—often referenced informally as “Velu AI” in commerce technology circles—is an AI-driven product data enrichment company founded in 2018 and headquartered in New York City, where it builds infrastructure to transform static ecommerce catalogs into self-improving product knowledge graphs for brands, retailers, and marketplaces.citeturn3search3turn3search2

## Core capabilities
- **High-fidelity enrichment:** Vertical-specific computer vision and natural language models claim up to 99% attribute precision, significantly outpacing generic tagging approaches.citeturn3search2
- **Agentic catalog automation:** Velou markets “sentient” or self-optimizing product catalogs that adapt content in real time based on performance signals, positioning the platform as product data infrastructure for the agent-first era of ecommerce.citeturn3search2turn2search7
- **Omnichannel activation:** More than 30 turnkey connectors stream enriched data into advertising, search, email, and operational stacks (including Shopify, Google, Meta, Algolia, and Klaviyo) while ingesting downstream signals to refine the catalog.citeturn3search2
- **Discovery use cases:** Structured catalog outputs are promoted for AI assistants, on-site search, merchandising, performance marketing, and marketplace feeds to reduce disapprovals and improve conversion.citeturn3search2

## Integrations and deployment notes
Velou emphasizes rapid deployment through one-click connectors and industry-tuned schemas, with documented integrations across SAP, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Shopify, BigCommerce, and other enterprise ecommerce systems to reduce manual data cleanup efforts.citeturn3search2turn3search11

- Align Velou’s enrichment outputs with existing product taxonomy governance to avoid clashes between local merchandising rules and Velou’s auto-generated attributes.citeturn3search2
- Evaluate downstream analytics changes in advertising, search, and marketplace channels once enriched feeds are activated, as Velou’s closed-loop signal ingestion can alter bidding strategies and content experiments.citeturn3search2turn2search7
